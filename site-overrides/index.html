About Notes archive

I am a first-year PhD student in the Department of Computer Science at The University of Manchester supervised by Dr. Michele Caprio and Prof. Samuel Kaski.

I received a B.Eng. in Electronic Information Engineering and an M.Eng. in Computer Science and Technology from the University of Electronic Science and Technology of China (UESTC). I had a wonderful time there. During my master's study, I was supervised by Prof. Yong Deng.

My research interests include optimal transport, imprecise probability, and uncertainty in AI. I also maintain a Note Archive, where I occasionally document ideas, reading notes, and things I learn along the way.
